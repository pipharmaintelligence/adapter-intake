# Evidence Policy

## Evidence classes

Use four explicit classes:

1. governed internal baseline;
2. grounded external evidence;
3. analysis or inference derived from evidence;
4. unresolved uncertainty or contradiction.

## Freshness

When timing changes the meaning of a claim, retain an as-of date or mark the date as unknown. Historical evidence must not be presented as current without qualification.

## Citation coverage

Claims introduced by grounded research must retain the bounded citation or reference information supplied by the trusted runtime projection. Analysis-only roles must not manufacture new citations.

## Contradictions

When two supported claims conflict, keep both in the evidence set, identify the contradiction, and prevent unqualified synthesis until the critic has classified the conflict.

## Missing evidence

A required subsection may explicitly state that evidence was not established. Missing evidence is preferable to fabricated completion.
